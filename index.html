About

About

I work on high-resolution simulations of fluid systems, with a focus on getting solvers to stay stable at the resolutions where the interesting physics shows up.

Most of my time goes into three things: writing solver code, running it on a cluster, and figuring out whether the output means anything. The last one is the hard part. Numerical results are easy to produce and easy to misread, so a lot of the work is validation — grid refinement studies, comparisons against benchmark cases, and checking that the quantities I care about converge for the right reasons.

Before this I studied mechanical engineering, which is where I picked up the habit of asking what a number is supposed to represent before trusting it.

Interests

What I’m working on

  • Direct numerical simulation

    Resolving the full range of scales in wall-bounded turbulence, without a subgrid model to hide behind.

  • Solver performance

    Getting time-to-solution down: better preconditioners, and communication patterns that survive scaling past a few hundred cores.

  • Verification and validation

    Convergence studies and benchmark comparisons, plus reproducible run configs so a result can be regenerated a year later.
